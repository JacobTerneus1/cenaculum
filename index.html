About

What is the Cenaculum?

The Cenaculum is a retreat where everything happens in Latin: liturgy, presentations, reflections, games, meals, walks, and conversations. Yep, for real. It's a gathering of people who actually love to speak Latin and come together for for a week to do that. At the Cenaculum we treat Latin as an active, living language, and we celebrate it as the language of the Catholic Church. The days are full of Latin activities and prayers but not hurried - it is indeed a true retreat, a time of rest and recuperation.

The Cenaculum is hosted by the Family of Saint Jerome at a beautiful retreat center run by the Institute of Christ the King in Burlington, WI.

De Cenaculo

Quid Cenaculum sit

Recessus omnino Latinus in quo omnia Latine aguntur: liturgia, orationes, meditationes, ludi, cenae, ambulationes, atque colloquia. Ita vero! In Cenaculo homines Latine colloqui amantes una conveniunt unam per hebdomadem ut Latine versentur. Apud Cenaculum Latinam activam et vivam Linguam censemus. Quoque veneremur hanc sicut lingam Ecclesiae Catholicae. Dies pleni sunt precationum agendorum sed nullo modo festinantes - verus recessus est, tempus quietis atque recuperationis.

Familia Sancti Hieronimi Cenaculum ducit apud campum amoenum Institutionis Christi Regis Burlingtoniae in Visconsia.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it fully in Latin?

Yes, the entire event is conducted entirely in Latin: talks, liturgy, meals, social conversations, etc. We ask participants to only speak Latin throughout the Cenaculum except in emergencies or matters of real necessity. Sometimes, of course, there is a good reason to use a word or two in another language. For instance, sometimes a talk will mention a Greek or Hebrew word. Or sometimes you won't know the Latin word for something, so you can judiciously ask "quomodo dicitur 'salad?'"

What does a day at Cenaculum look like?

A typical day includes Prime, Mass, breakfast, two conference talks, breaks, Rosary, Confessions, and the Angelus, lunch, midday rest, games, a brief talk, common reading, Vespers, dinner, a walk, evening recreation, and Compline.

What should I pack?

In addition to the clothes, hygiene items, medications, and other personal necessities for a week-long stay, you will likely want to bring a rosary, a notebook, a pen, a Latin dictionary, and perhaps a Latin book you would enjoy reading during quiet breaks. In addition, we customarily put on a low-key talent show on the final evening, so consider any props or musical instrument you might need for your talent.

What if I have dietary restrictions or food allergies?

For special dietary needs, you will have a chance to indicate the details on the registration form. You can also reach out to Abbe Jeffrey at retreatcenter@icksp.org with more detailed questions regarding food.

FAQ

Quaestiones communes

Estne omnino Latine?

Ita, totum eventum Latine peragitur: sermones, liturgia, prandia, colloquia socialia, et cetera. Rogamus participes ut per totum Cenaculum Latine tantum loquantur, nisi in extremis aut in rebus valde necessariis. Interdum quidem est bona causa uno alterove verbo lingua alia uti. Exempli gratia, aliquando sermo vocabulum Graecum vel Hebraicum memorabit. Vel aliquando vocabulum Latinum rei nescies, et tunc prudenter quaerere potes: "quomodo dicitur 'salad'?"

Quid est horarium cotidianum?

Dies typicus includit Primam, Missam, ientaculum, duas conferentias, quietes, Rosarium, Confessiones et Angelum, prandium, meridiationem, ludos, sessiunculam brevem, lectionem communem, Vesperas, cenam, deambulationem, delectationem vespertinam, atque Completorium.

Quid mecum ferre debeo?

Praeter vestes, res ad munditiam pertinentes, medicamenta, et alias necessitates personales pro hebdomada, probabiliter ferre voles rosarium, codicillum, calamum, lexicon Latinum, atque fortasse librum Latinum quem per quietas pausas libenter legeris. Praeterea, more nostro, in ultima vespera moderatum spectaculum talentorum habemus; itaque cogita de quibusvis rebus vel instrumento musico quibus opus sit ad talentum tuum exhibendum.

Quid si restrictiones cibarias vel allergias habeo?

Pro necessitatibus cibariis peculiares, in formulario inscriptionis eas indicare poteris. Etiam ad Abbatem Jeffrey per retreatcenter@icksp.org cum quaestionibus accuratioribus de cibo scribere potes.
