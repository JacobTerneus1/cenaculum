Supplementary Reading

Pius XII on Media, Truth, and Peace

The gifts, advantages, and utilities offered by every kind of technical discipline cannot contribute to man's prosperity and true happiness unless they foster the goods of the soul, obey the norms of nature, and submit entirely to the eternal laws of God.

Broadcasting can spread truths or errors, virtues or crimes, concord or discord, brotherly charity or deadly hatred far and wide. How important it is, then, that those who are Christian, or even simply upright in mind, should take the greatest care in this matter; and above all that they should strive with all their might to ensure that the speaking boxes now found in many homes do not turn minds away from truth through the painted appearance of error, do not lure souls toward vice by the enticements of deceptive art, but rather commend virtue, spread sound doctrine, refresh their hearers with wholesome delights, and lighten the cares that so sharply trouble and burden men today.

Why then should the Church not use this most powerful aid to carry out her mission more effectively and more easily, that mission which Jesus Christ commanded to the Apostles and to those who succeeded them: "Preach the Gospel to every creature"?

We, therefore, once again raise our fatherly voice, urging peace and brotherly concord upon all peoples, nations, and men. ... We call this a just peace, that fairest gift of God, and we invite all once again to strengthen and confirm it.

Lectio Suppletoria

Pius XII de Instrumentis, Veritate, et Pace

... ea, quae technicae omne genus disciplinae dant munera, commoda, utilitates, minime posse ad hominum prosperitatem verique nominis felicitatem conferre, nisi bona animi, quae potiora sunt, provehant, nisi naturae normis obtemperent, nisi denique aeterni Dei legibus omnino oboediant.

... radiophonicam rem quod attinet, quisnam ignorat eam posse veritates vel errores, virtutes vel flagitia, concordiam vel discordiam, fraternam caritatem vel exitiale odium usquequaque propagare ac disseminare? Quantum igitur interest ... ut undisona scrinia, quae hodie in multis domibus sunt, non a veritate, per fucatam erroris speciem, avertant mentes, non ad vitia, fallacis artis illecebris ornata, animos alliciant, sed virtutem potius suadeant, sed rectam doctrinam propagent, sed sanis quoque oblectamentis auditores reficiant ...

Cur igitur Ecclesia potentissimo hoc adiumento non utatur ad munus suum expeditius obeundum ... "Praedicate Evangelium omni creaturae."

Nos igitur ... paternam iterum attollimus vocem, fraternam omnibus pacem suadentes. ... Nos ... iustam dicimus pacem, Dei munus pulcherrimum ... iterum atque iterum ad eam solidandam confirmandamque invitamus omnes.
